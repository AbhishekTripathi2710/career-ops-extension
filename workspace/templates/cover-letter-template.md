**[Paragraph 1: The Hook & Core Specialization]**
Start by introducing your target role and expressing direct alignment. Hook the reader immediately with your core specialization (e.g. "AI Software Engineer specializing in visual document intelligence and robust backend pipelines"). Connect your background to their current engineering goals (e.g., scaling transaction volume or improving information retrieval accuracy).

**[Paragraph 2: The Proof Point & Metric Impact]**
Elaborate on a key project or experience that matches their tech stack. Walk through a specific technical challenge using a STAR narrative: what the problem was (e.g. scrambled RAG outputs on multi-column layouts), the action you took (e.g. implementing visual parser models and RAGAS evaluations), and the direct quantifiable outcome (e.g. boosting answer relevance by 30% across 1,000+ files). 

**[Paragraph 3: Team Value & Call to Action]**
Describe why this company's technology or mission appeals to you. Connect this to your own growth path. Conclude with a polite call to action expressing interest in discussing how your skills can help resolve their upcoming engineering challenges.
